Method

Three steps, no migration

01
Connect the mailbox you already use
Gmail, Microsoft 365, Yahoo, or any IMAP server — multiple accounts, searched as one. Your mail server, your credentials, nothing rehosted.
02
Cortex reads, scores, and remembers
Every thread is indexed locally in SQLite. Urgency, commitments, relationship health, and open loops are computed continuously — not on request.
03
You act on signal, not on volume
Focus tab for what's urgent today. Weekly Brief for what mattered this week. A drafted reply, in your voice, waiting when you need one.

Field report

What's actually in the build

Triage & drafting 7-signal score

The part that reads everything so you don't have to.

  • Smart Draft writes in your voice, learned from your own sent mail.
  • Pre-send review flags unanswered questions and commitments before you hit send.
  • Send-Time Optimizer tells you the hour this recipient actually replies.

Relationship intelligence VIP · 0–100

The part that remembers people, not just threads.

  • Client Health Score and Escalation Radar catch a relationship going quiet before it costs you one.
  • Commitment Tracker scans every thread for what you owe and what's owed to you.
  • Contact Tracker merges duplicates, imports vCard/CSV, syncs Microsoft 365.

Executive reporting daily · weekly

The part you forward to no one, because it's already for you.

  • Weekly Brief and Morning Brief — decisions made, who's waiting, what's due.
  • Role Transition Briefing summarizes your entire corpus for a first week in a new seat.
  • Knowledge hub — People Graph, Open Loops, AI Clusters, Topic Timeline.

Every channel, one inbox email · sms · social

The part that stopped pretending email was the only channel.

  • SMS via Twilio, unified alongside email — polled, not webhooked, so nothing needs a public server.
  • Social Inbox for LinkedIn and Instagram DMs, plus an autopilot review queue for outbound posts.
  • CRM Pipeline, Job Tracker, and Meeting Intelligence for the work that starts in email and doesn't end there.
